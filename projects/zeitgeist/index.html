Zeitgeist

Research into what people are paying attention to right now, and what that attention is connected to, built on Wikipedia pageviews and clickstream data.

Year
2026
Status
In progress
Role
Solo research
Made with
Data, Python, Machine learning, Research

A research project with a small product around the parts that held up. Using free, back-testable data (Wikipedia pageviews and clickstream, GDELT news), it asks two questions: what is breaking out now, and what non-obvious concepts is the audience for a topic also looking at.

Nowcasting works as a detector of current breakouts, though not as a forecast. The association engine is the real result: links predicted from past reading behaviour were validated forward in time (AUC 0.89), and it surfaces affinities a keyword tool would miss. Predicting the next trend did not work at any scale, and the write-up says so. There is an API and an interactive map of a quarter of a million concepts.
