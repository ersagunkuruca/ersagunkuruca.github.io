Doppler Plane Listener

Point your mic at a passing plane and read its speed and closest-approach distance from the Doppler shift — log-frequency cross-correlation drives an evidence grid over (speed × distance).

Open it

A plane on a straight, level track produces a Doppler sweep — the pitch glides down through its true tone at closest approach. This listens through your mic, resamples the spectrum onto a log-frequency axis (where a Doppler shift becomes a sideways slide), cross-correlates successive frames to recover the slide rate, and folds that into a log-odds evidence grid over speed × tangent distance. The bright cell is the live estimate; a “simulate pass” mode works with no plane needed. Pure JavaScript and the Web Audio API.
