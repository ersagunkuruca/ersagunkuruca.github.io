A Wear OS watch face where telling the time means reading color. Each hour’s hue advances 5/12 of a turn around the wheel — a step coprime with 12, so consecutive hours land far apart and all twelve hues are visited before the cycle repeats. The minute isn’t a hand at all: it’s the sweeping edge of fresh paint being laid over the dial. The whole face is just two static gradient layers, so it costs almost nothing to run.
