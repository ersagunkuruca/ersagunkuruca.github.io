Gecko Desktop Pet

A procedurally animated 3D gecko that climbs around your desktop in a transparent, click-through window, built to put the IK work to real use.

Year
2026
Status
In progress
Role
Solo build
Made with
Unity, C#, Animation, Inverse kinematics

A desktop pet that treats the screen as a wall. The creature walks with analytic two-bone IK on four legs and a diagonal plant-and-step gait, wanders on its own, walks to the cursor on request and reacts when poked. It lives in a full-screen, always-on-top, per-pixel transparent window that only catches clicks over the gecko itself, so it never gets in the way.

An early prototype: the hard parts (the overlay window and the walking rig) work, and the art is still placeholder. It grew out of the IK Solver experiments.
