Pitch Field

Live polyphonic pitch detection in the browser: a Constant-Q transform feeds competing pitch hypotheses that explain away shared harmonics — rejecting phantom octaves with no thresholds or peak-picking.

Open it

Polyphonic pitch detection framed as competing beliefs. The mic feeds a Constant-Q transform (5-cent bins); each candidate fundamental gathers its harmonics, then pitches explain away the partials they share. That competition is what kills phantom octaves and sub-harmonics — no thresholds, no peak-picking, no hand-tuned rules — and the resolution is fine enough to tell a played major third from a bass note’s 5th harmonic, 14 cents apart. Pure JavaScript and the Web Audio API, no build step.

It plays chord buttons, or listens through your microphone.
