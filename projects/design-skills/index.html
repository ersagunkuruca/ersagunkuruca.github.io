Design Skills for Claude Code

Two skills that teach a coding agent to design like a person who chooses: one for the method, one for showcase effects, rebuilt after measuring where the agent's designs converged.

Year
2026
Status
In progress
Role
Solo research
Made with
Design, AI agents, CSS, WebGL

Generated interfaces tend to share a handful of looks. This project measured that: the same eight briefs were designed with and without a design skill, and a script read the rendered styles of every page (share of rounded boxes and pills, fonts, accents, borders, shadows). Both versions converged, just on different defaults.

The rebuilt skills replace ban lists with choices. modern-ui-design makes the agent decide what each page is for (recede, operate or impress), pick a school of design that fits the subject, sketch three different directions, and render and measure the result. web-showcase-effects covers the impress side: shaders, scroll scenes, live-HTML effects and the guardrails that keep them accessible. This site was redesigned with them.
