Work

Games, apps, web toys and research. Some shipped, some live in the browser, some being brought back.

Show
  1. 2026 Circle of Fifths — Watch Face A Wear OS watch face where the clock is a field of color, not hands — each hour's hue steps around the color wheel. In progress
  2. 2026 Design Skills for Claude Code Two skills that teach a coding agent to design like a person who chooses: one for the method, one for showcase effects, rebuilt after measuring where the agent's designs converged. In progress
  3. 2026 Dither Lab An image-dithering playground — Floyd–Steinberg, Atkinson, and ordered dithering with retro palettes and proper sRGB-vs-linear handling. Live
  4. 2026 Doppler Plane Listener Point your mic at a passing plane and read its speed and closest-approach distance from the Doppler shift — log-frequency cross-correlation drives an evidence grid over (speed × distance). Live
  5. 2026 Gecko Desktop Pet A procedurally animated 3D gecko that climbs around your desktop in a transparent, click-through window, built to put the IK work to real use. In progress
  6. 2026 Grapher A Desmos-style parametric grapher with a real symbolic-math engine — AST parsing, differentiation, and Newton's method — plus a hierarchical equation editor. Live
  7. 2026 HSI Playground A WebGPU playground for the HSI colour model — comparing CSS HSL, mean-intensity HSI, and a luminance-based variant, with the tilted RGB-cube slice rendered in 3D. Live
  8. 2026 Kablammo — Watch Face A Wear OS watch face that animates the Kablammo variable font's axis — in a format (WFF) that has no variable-font animation. In progress
  9. 2026 KilnWatch A ceramics-firing companion: log time and temperature during a kiln firing, segment it into ramp / soak / cooling phases, and chart the curve. Live
  10. 2026 Language Lab Experiments in new programming-language ideas: a projectional language whose source is a tree with names kept on the side, and functions that run in any direction. Live
  11. 2026 Pitch Field Live polyphonic pitch detection in the browser: a Constant-Q transform feeds competing pitch hypotheses that explain away shared harmonics — rejecting phantom octaves with no thresholds or peak-picking. Live
  12. 2026 Scale Coverage Explorer An interactive explorer of all 2,048 scales of 12-tone equal temperament, sorted by how much chord-coverage each provides. Live
  13. 2026 Shop Kit A template for Turkish online shops on Medusa and Next.js, with the local pieces Medusa doesn't ship: iyzico payments, cargo, e-invoices, a point-of-sale page and distributor catalogs. In progress
  14. 2026 WebGL Waveform A hand-written WebGL visualizer: glowing, seamless waveform lines drawn as triangle strips, with instanced particles. Live
  15. 2026 Zeitgeist Research into what people are paying attention to right now, and what that attention is connected to, built on Wikipedia pageviews and clickstream data. In progress
  16. 2025 IK Solver Inverse-kinematics solvers for Unity — an analytic solver, physics-based solvers, and humanoid foot-IK. In progress
  17. 2022 Bubblez A colorful bubble-shooter for mobile, published under Velvet Games. Released · currently unavailable
  18. 2020 Slime Orbit An arcade space-shooter published under Velvet Games. Released · currently unavailable
  19. 2019 beybisi A companion app for pregnant women and new moms, published under Velvet Games. Released · currently unavailable
