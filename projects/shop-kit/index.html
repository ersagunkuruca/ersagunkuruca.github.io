Shop Kit

A template for Turkish online shops on Medusa and Next.js, with the local pieces Medusa doesn't ship: iyzico payments, cargo, e-invoices, a point-of-sale page and distributor catalogs.

Year
2026
Status
In progress
Role
Design & development
Made with
E-commerce, TypeScript, Medusa, Next.js

Clone it, edit two shop folders and the environment, and you have a shop. On top of Medusa v2 and a Next.js 15 storefront it adds the parts a Turkish retailer needs: iyzico card payments, cargo and tracking through Geliver, e-Arşiv and e-Fatura invoices through Nilvera, distributor catalogs that import XML or Excel feeds and reprice automatically, a Kasa (point of sale) page with barcode scanning and label printing, and a sales ledger with profit reports. The plugins are versioned packages, so every shop built from the template takes fixes by bumping a version. The first shop built on it is in testing.
